About OnPoint Nurse & Home Care

Clinical excellence, personal dignity, and family-centered elder care across Metro Vancouver, BC.

Our Founding Purpose

Redefining Elder Care With Compassion & Clinical Leadership

OnPoint Nurse and Home Care was founded to bridge a vital need across Metro Vancouver (Vancouver, Richmond, Burnaby, Surrey & surrounding communities): providing families with professional, clinically supervised healthcare for aging parents without taking them away from the warmth and familiarity of their homes.

We understand the delicate balance families navigate. You want your parents to feel respected and comfortable in their own home, but you also need confidence that their physical health, cognitive changes, nutrition, and daily safety are overseen by trained healthcare professionals.

Dignity First

Preserving self-worth, respect, and autonomy at every stage of aging.

Clinical Rigor

Led by registered nurses with over 20 years of acute and community nursing experience in British Columbia.

Family Alliance

Partnering openly with adult children and family caregivers to ensure holistic wellbeing.

"Our mission is to help seniors live comfortably with dignity while relieving families of anxiety through reliable clinical support."

— Risper Murunga, RN, BSN, MPH
Our Guiding Principle

Care That Looks at the Whole Person

Cognitive & Emotional Health

Stimulating memory gently, encouraging storytelling, and reducing feelings of isolation or agitation.

Mobility & Physical Safety

Proactive environmental hazard identification, transfer support, and fall prevention.

Nutrition & Daily Living

Appetizing, culturally appropriate meals, hydration monitoring, and dignified hygiene routines.

Medical & Nursing Supervision

Vitals observation, medication compliance, wound care, and close doctor communication.
