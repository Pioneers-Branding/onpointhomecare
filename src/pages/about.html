About OnPoint Nurse & Home Care

Clinical excellence, personal dignity, and family-centered elder care in Kenya.

Our Founding Purpose

Redefining Elder Care With Compassion & Clinical Leadership

OnPoint Nurse and Home Care was founded to bridge a vital need in Kenya: providing families with professional, clinically supervised healthcare for aging parents without taking them away from the warmth and familiarity of their homes.

We understand the delicate balance families navigate. You want your parents to feel respected and comfortable in their own home, but you also need confidence that their physical health, cognitive changes, nutrition, and daily safety are overseen by trained healthcare professionals.

💙

Dignity First

Preserving self-worth, respect, and autonomy at every stage of aging.

🩺

Clinical Rigor

Led by registered nurses with over 20 years of acute and community nursing experience.

🏡

Family Alliance

Partnering openly with adult children and caregivers to ensure holistic wellbeing.
