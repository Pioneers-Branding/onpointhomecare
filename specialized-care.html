Clinical Expertise

Targeted Solutions for Life's Most Critical Health Transitions

Generic home care agencies often attempt a one-size-fits-all approach to elder care. But recovering from a broken hip requires a completely different clinical protocol than managing sundowning dementia or transitioning home after major surgery.

OnPoint’s Specialized Care pillar offers tailored, condition-specific pathways developed by nursing leaders. We integrate environmental safety audits, medication reconciliation, proactive vital surveillance, and compassionate personal care to keep seniors safe, comfortable, and independent in their own homes.

"Specialized care means matching the right clinical skill and caregiver temperament to the exact challenges your family faces today."

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions About Specialized Care

Our specialized care programs are designed and supervised directly by our Lead Registered Nurse (BSN, MPH, 20+ years experience). Every pathway—from post-hospital transitions and fall recovery to complex chronic disease management—incorporates clinical risk assessments, physician synchronization, and dedicated primary caregivers.

You don't have to figure it out alone. During our free in-home clinical assessment, our Lead Registered Nurse evaluates mobility, medications, cognition, and home safety to recommend an exact, tailored care pathway.

Standard specialized care can start within 24–48 hours. In acute situations, such as unexpected hospital discharge or sudden family caregiver injury, we offer same-day urgent deployment across Metro Vancouver.

Yes. Most specialized care pathways blend clinical nursing procedures (such as wound dressing or medication titration) with daily personal care aide assistance for bathing, transfers, and meal preparation.

Our registered nurses meet the patient at the hospital bedside, conduct medication reconciliation with the discharging doctor, arrange transportation, prepare the home, and oversee initial recovery shifts.

Yes. All care aides and nurses are trained in biomechanics, ceiling and floor mechanical lifts (Hoyer), slide sheets, and fall prevention protocols.

Yes. Many extended health benefit policies, long-term care insurance, ICBC claims (for motor vehicle accident recoveries), and Veterans Affairs Canada cover our specialized care.

Every specialized care client is supervised by our Lead Registered Nurse with regular vital sign tracking, scheduled reassessments, and direct communication with family doctors.
