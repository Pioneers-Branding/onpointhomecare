Secure Patient Referral Portal

Fast-track clinical referral submission for physicians, hospital discharge teams, and healthcare coordinators across Burnaby, Surrey, New Westminster & Richmond.

Triage SLA < 2-Hour Response
Bedside Intake Hospital Visits Available
Data Privacy BC PIPA Compliant
Coverage Area Metro Vancouver

Step 1: Submit Form

Fill out the clinical referral form below with patient acuity, discharge target, and family contact details.

Step 2: 2-Hour Triage

Our Lead RN reviews the file, confirms service availability, and initiates contact with the patient's family.

Step 3: Seamless Start

Care begins with doorstep reception, bedside discharge assistance, and structured physician debriefs.

Clinical Scope

Supported High-Acuity Clinical Procedures

Our Registered Nurses and Licensed Practical Nurses execute advanced hospital-grade directives at home:

Central Line & IV Therapy

PICC line, Hickman, and Port-a-Cath maintenance, sterile dressing changes, heparin/saline flushes, IV antibiotic administration, and TPN management.

Complex Wound Care & NPWT

Negative pressure wound therapy (VAC systems), surgical dehiscence management, stage 3–4 pressure injury care, and compression therapy.

Enteral Feeding & Airway Care

G-tube/J-tube feed administration, pump programming, stoma skin barrier maintenance, tracheostomy suctioning, and inner cannula care.

Catheter & Ostomy Management

Indwelling Foley and suprapubic catheter insertions and irrigations, nephrostomy drainage management, and colostomy/ileostomy appliance care.
