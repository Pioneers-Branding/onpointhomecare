Independent Home Care & Daily Living Support

Discreet, autonomy-preserving assistance that empowers older adults to maintain their independence, lifestyle, and comfort safely at home.

Care Objective Maximizing Autonomy
Shift Flexibility A Few Hours to Daily Check-ins
Caregiver Model Familiar Primary Aide
Service Area Metro Vancouver
Empowered Aging

Support That Enhances Independence, Never Takes It Away

Most seniors cherish their independence above all else. They want to remain in the home where their memories were made, prepare their favorite meals, follow their own schedules, and welcome visitors on their own terms. However, subtle changes in balance, stamina, or nutrition can place that independence at risk.

OnPoint’s Independent Home Care provides the perfect balance of respectful support and clinical safety. We handle the tasks that cause fatigue or injury—such as heavy laundry, deep grocery shopping, bathtub transfers, and medication organization—allowing your parent to focus on what they enjoy most.

"Our goal is never to take over your parent's life, but to be the invisible safety net that keeps them independent, thriving, and in control."

Everyday Independence

Key Elements of Independent Home Support

Targeted assistance designed to keep daily life comfortable, safe, and stress-free.

Nutritious Meal Prep & Hydration

Cooking fresh, wholesome meals tailored to dietary requirements (low-sodium, diabetic, cardiac), pre-portioning lunches, and encouraging healthy fluid intake.

Medication Reminders & Setup

Ensuring prescription blister packs are taken on time, tracking refills with the pharmacy, and preventing double-dosing or missed vital medications.

Light Housekeeping & Fall Mitigation

Changing heavy bed linens, keeping walkways clear, sanitizing kitchen and bathroom surfaces, taking out the garbage, and clearing clutter.

Safety Check-Ins & Family Connectivity

Regular daily or weekly visits to verify physical well-being, provide uplifting companionship, assist with video calls to grandchildren, and report to family.

The Independence Plan

How We Set Up Your Independent Care Plan

A personalized, respectful approach tailored to your exact daily routine.

Stage 01

Autonomy & Lifestyle Alignment

Our Lead Registered Nurse meets with your parent to understand their preferred schedule, morning habits, favorite meals, and the specific daily tasks where a helping hand would bring the greatest relief.

  • Zero imposition — the client dictates their schedule and boundaries
  • Identification of hidden fall hazards or tiring heavy chores
  • Customization of dietary preferences and shopping lists
Stage 02

Caregiver Matching & Mutual Chemistry

We pair your parent with a compatible, respectful caregiver whose personality and background create a natural, enjoyable dynamic.

  • Shared interests, temperament, and cultural background matching
  • Consistency guarantee with a primary dedicated caregiver
  • Warm, respectful, and unobtrusive professional etiquette
Stage 03

Flexible, Routine-Based Shifts

Whether assistance is needed for 3 hours twice a week or daily morning visits, your caregiver arrives punctually and assists with warmth and efficiency.

  • Fresh home-cooked meals prepared and stored for later
  • Assistance with laundry, grocery shopping, and light tidying
  • Gentle walking accompaniment for fresh air and exercise
Stage 04

Proactive RN Safety Net

Behind the scenes, our Registered Nurse monitors health trends and is always on standby should illness or surgery require clinical support.

  • Early detection of subtle mobility, cognitive, or weight changes
  • Seamless escalation to skilled nursing if required
  • Peace of mind for adult children living across the city or abroad
