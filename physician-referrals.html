Clinical Partnership

Your Trusted Eyes and Ears in the Patient's Home

Managing complex geriatric, post-surgical, or multi-morbid patients in a 15-minute clinic visit is challenging. Clinicians often lack clear visibility into whether patients are taking their medications correctly, maintaining adequate hydration, or experiencing silent physical decline.

OnPoint Nurse & Home Care acts as a direct extension of your practice. Led by Risper Murunga, RN, BSN, MPH, our clinical team implements your treatment plans, monitors vital trends, and alerts you early to subtle clinical shifts—long before they escalate into an emergency department visit.

"We give physicians complete clinical confidence that medical orders are executed accurately and that patient progress is tracked rigorously at home."

Clinical Synergies

Why Primary Care & Specialist Physicians Refer to OnPoint

Structured medical support designed to improve patient adherence and clinical outcomes:

Closed-Loop Clinical Charting

Receive bi-weekly nursing progress notes, vital sign logs (BP, SpO2, glucose, weight), and wound measurement records transmitted directly to your clinic.

Medication Compliance Oversight

Eliminate missed doses and double-dosing. Our nurses reconcile complex regimens with dispensing pharmacies and verify daily blister-pack compliance.

Chronic Disease Surveillance

Proactive monitoring for CHF (daily weight, edema), COPD (dyspnea, oxygenation), and Diabetes (glucometry, sliding scale insulin, skin checks).

Clinic Escort & Note-Taking

Our Senior Escort caregivers accompany your frail patients directly to their clinic visits with you, taking notes and relaying instructions to family.

Disease Pathways

In-Home Clinical Surveillance for Complex Patients

Specialized nursing protocols that keep multi-morbid patients medically stable in the community:

Protocol 01

Congestive Heart Failure (CHF) & Cardiac Surveillance

Daily dry weight logs, pedal edema grading, orthopnea checks, strict sodium-controlled meal prep, and immediate physician notification for weight gains >2 lbs in 24 hours.

  • Diuretic timing and fluid restriction adherence monitoring
  • Resting heart rate, blood pressure, and pulse oximetry tracking
  • Coordination with regional Heart Function Clinics
Protocol 02

Type 1 & 2 Diabetes Glycemic Management

Licensed RN/LPN blood glucose testing, sliding scale insulin administration, hypoglycemia prevention, and routine diabetic foot inspections to prevent neuropathic ulcers.

  • Glycemic trend logs transmitted for quarterly HbA1c reviews
  • Carbohydrate-managed meal planning and hydration routines
  • Diabetic skin barrier and pressure relief care
Protocol 03

COPD, Asthma & Respiratory Disease

SpO2 monitoring, oxygen concentrator checks, nebulizer/metered-dose inhaler compliance, and energy conservation coaching during personal care.

  • Sputum color and volume tracking for early detection of bacterial exacerbations
  • Paced breathing and pursed-lip ventilation support
  • Emergency action plan triggers for acute dyspnea
