Complex Medical Care at Home

Advanced, clinical nursing and multidisciplinary support for high-acuity chronic conditions across Metro Vancouver.

Acuity Level Advanced & Complex Care
Staffing Model RN & LPN Clinical Teams
Medical Devices G-Tube, Catheter, O2 & BiPAP
Service Area Burnaby, Surrey, New Westminster & Richmond
Hospital-Grade Home Nursing

Managing Intensive Clinical Needs Outside the Hospital Ward

When a senior lives with multiple chronic diseases, progressive neurological disorders, or complex medical equipment, family members are often told that long-term institutional care is their only option. At OnPoint, we believe that with experienced clinical leadership and specialized nursing, high-acuity care can be safely delivered in the comfort of home.

Our Complex Care at Home service brings hospital-caliber clinical expertise into your living room. From enteral tube feeding (PEG/G-tube) and indwelling Foley catheter maintenance to supplemental oxygen monitoring, tracheostomy care, and complex multi-drug regimens, our licensed nurses provide rigorous, compassionate oversight around the clock.

"High medical acuity does not have to mean a hospital room. We deliver intensive, evidence-based nursing care right at the bedside."

Clinical Scope

Complex Medical Conditions We Frequently Manage

High-level care customized to individual diagnostic profiles:

Neurological & Motor Disorders

Advanced Parkinson's disease, Amyotrophic Lateral Sclerosis (ALS), Multiple Sclerosis, and severe post-stroke hemiplegia.

Advanced Cardiopulmonary Diseases

Congestive Heart Failure (CHF Stage III/IV), severe COPD requiring continuous oxygen/BiPAP, and pulmonary fibrosis.

Enteral Nutrition & Feeding Tubes

Gastrostomy (G-tube) and Jejunostomy (J-tube) formula administration, flush protocols, site dressing, and pump management.

Catheter & Ostomy Management

Indwelling urethral (Foley) and suprapubic catheters, colostomy/ileostomy appliance maintenance, and skin barrier protection.

Clinical Procedures

What Our Complex Care Nursing Delivers

Rigorous medical protocols executed by Registered Nurses and Licensed Practical Nurses.

Procedure 01

Enteral Nutrition & Feeding Tube Care (PEG / G-Tube)

Our nurses oversee enteral formula administration, pump programming, scheduled water flushes, medication crushing and delivery through the tube, and sterile stoma site cleansing.

  • Continuous and bolus enteral feeding administration
  • Stoma site skin inspection, cleansing, and dressing changes
  • Troubleshooting tube clogs and verifying retention balloon volume
  • Aspiration prevention and head-of-bed positioning protocols
Procedure 02

Respiratory Support, Oxygen Therapy & Suctioning

Managing supplemental oxygen concentrators, BiPAP/CPAP non-invasive ventilation, nebulizer treatments, oral-pharyngeal suctioning, and respiratory distress triage.

  • Continuous SpO2 and respiratory rate surveillance
  • Aseptic suctioning and oral hygiene maintenance
  • Nebulized bronchodilator and steroid administration
  • Emergency oxygen titration protocols under physician orders
Procedure 03

Indwelling Catheter & Urological Care

Sterile insertion, scheduled bag changes, catheter irrigation, and vigilant monitoring to eliminate catheter-associated urinary tract infections (CAUTIs).

  • Sterile catheter insertion, replacement, and anchoring
  • Urine output volume, color, and sediment tracking
  • Early identification of urosepsis symptoms and prompt escalation
  • Suprapubic catheter site cleansing and dressing management
Procedure 04

Advanced Wound & Pressure Injury Management

Clinical staging, sterile debridement monitoring, NPWT (wound vac) maintenance, and advanced dressing changes for Stage 3/4 pressure injuries and diabetic ulcers.

  • Wound measurement, photography, and healing rate documentation
  • Specialized hydrocolloid, alginate, and foam dressing changes
  • Negative pressure wound therapy (wound vac) operation
  • Strict Q2H repositioning schedules to prevent new tissue breakdown
Procedure 05

Complex Polypharmacy & Subcutaneous Injections

Accurate administration of complex multi-drug schedules, insulin titration, anticoagulant injections (e.g. Lovenox), and monitoring for toxicities or interactions.

  • Subcutaneous, intramuscular, and oral medication delivery
  • Blood glucose testing and sliding-scale insulin administration
  • Pain assessment scoring and opioid administration tracking
  • Direct coordination with prescribers for dosage adjustments
Procedure 06

Mechanical Lift Transfers & Bed Mobility

Providing safe, two-person mechanical Hoyer lift transfers, ceiling track operation, and gentle repositioning for individuals with severe mobility impairments.

  • Certified mechanical lift operation with zero risk of strain
  • Pressure-relieving mattress setup and repositioning
  • Gentle passive range-of-motion (ROM) exercises to prevent contractures
  • Skin integrity inspections over all bony prominences
"Complex clinical care is not just about operating medical devices; it is about bringing calm, human compassion to individuals facing acute health challenges."

Common Questions About Complex Care at Home
