Clinical Excellence

Home Care Directed by Experienced Nursing Leadership

Most home care agencies in British Columbia are business franchises run by operators without healthcare backgrounds. When health emergencies arise, subtle infection signs emerge, or medications conflict, non-clinical agencies often lack the expertise to act proactively.

OnPoint is fundamentally different. Founded and actively directed by Risper Murunga (RN, BSN, MPH), our care plans are built on decades of acute hospital and community health experience. Every caregiver is clinically trained, every shift is monitored, and every family receives direct access to registered nurse guidance.

"When you trust us with your parent, you are not just getting a companion; you are getting a clinical safety net that protects their health, dignity, and independence."

— Risper Murunga, Clinical Director

Our Core Pillars

Four Pillars of the OnPoint Standard

1. Registered Nurse Clinical Leadership

Founded by Risper Murunga (RN, BSN, MPH), OnPoint approaches every care plan with clinical rigor. We catch subtle physiological changes, medication interactions, and infection signs before they escalate.

2. Zero Stranger Rotation

We know how disorienting it is for an aging parent to have different people entering their home each week. We pair your loved one with a dedicated primary caregiver who becomes a trusted family companion.

3. Holistic, Whole-Person Care

We care for the human being, not just the diagnosis. From preserving cherished cultural recipes and hobbies to protecting dignity during personal hygiene, our care nurtures emotional and physical well-being.

4. Transparent Family Communication

Whether you live down the street in Vancouver or across the world, you receive digital shift summaries, vital updates, and direct access to our nursing supervisors.
