Disease Stabilization

Replacing Emergency Room Visits with Consistent Home Stability

Living with chronic diseases like Congestive Heart Failure, COPD, Diabetes, or Hypertension often feels like walking a tightrope. A missed diuretic dose, a high-sodium meal, or an unnoticed respiratory infection can trigger a sudden physical decompensation and a traumatic trip to the emergency room.

OnPoint transforms chronic disease management from reactive crisis response into calm, predictable stability. Led by Risper Murunga, RN, BSN, MPH, our nursing team conducts daily biometric surveillance, oversees medication compliance, prepares condition-specific meals, and communicates regularly with your doctor.

"Chronic conditions don't have to define your parent's quality of life. With meticulous daily nursing surveillance, we keep health parameters stable and keep families out of the hospital."

Clinical Protocols

Supported Chronic Conditions

Specialized clinical protocols tailored to the unique physiological requirements of each diagnosis:

Congestive Heart Failure (CHF)

Daily morning weight logs, pedal edema inspection, low-sodium meal prep, fluid restriction tracking, and early diuretic adjustment coordination.

COPD & Respiratory Disease

Continuous SpO2 pulse oximetry, home oxygen equipment safety checks, inhaler and nebulizer compliance, and energy conservation coaching.

Type 1 & 2 Diabetes Care

Daily blood glucose tracking, sliding-scale insulin administration by licensed nurses, diabetic neuropathy foot checks, and low-glycemic meal planning.

Hypertension & Renal Disease

Calibrated blood pressure surveillance, renal-safe low potassium/phosphorus diet preparation, medication timing, and lab requisition tracking.

Clinical Metrics

Biometric Surveillance & Action Parameters

Clear clinical thresholds monitored by our Registered Nurses to intercept disease flare-ups:

Blood Pressure & Heart Rate

Calibrated automatic oscillometric BP cuffs with standardized seated readings, postural orthostatic checks, and immediate physician escalation for SBP >160 or <90 mmHg.

Daily Dry Weight (CHF)

Morning post-void weights logged before breakfast on calibrated digital scales. Tracking fluid accumulation to trigger diuretic titration before pulmonary congestion develops.

Pulse Oximetry & Respiratory Rate

Continuous SpO2 measurement with fingertip sensors, dyspnea grading during exertion, and supplemental oxygen flow rate verification according to physician directives.

Glucometry & Insulin Administration

Fasting and pre-prandial blood glucose logs, hypoglycemic symptom checks (diaphoresis, tremors), sliding-scale insulin delivery by licensed nurses, and emergency glucagon readiness.
