Pricing Clarity

Honest, Upfront Pricing to Help Your Family Plan Confidently

One of the most stressful parts of arranging elder care is finding clear, transparent pricing. Many agencies hide their rates behind long sales calls or lock families into rigid monthly contracts with surprise administrative fees.

At OnPoint, we believe in complete financial transparency. We provide itemized rate breakdowns, flexible shift options, and clear guidance on how to leverage Canadian tax credits, extended health benefits, and government programs to make premium nurse-led care sustainable for your family.

Offsetting Your Care Costs

Financial Relief, Insurance & Tax Credits

Many families can offset a significant portion of private home care costs through Canadian tax programs and benefits.

Medical Expense Tax Credit (METC)

Under the Canada Revenue Agency (CRA), attendant care and nursing expenses can be claimed as medical expenses, delivering substantial tax deductions.

Disability Tax Credit (DTC)

Qualifying seniors with severe and prolonged impairments in walking, memory, or daily living can access significant non-refundable tax credits.

Extended Health & Private Insurance

Many employer extended health plans and private Long-Term Care (LTC) policies cover private nursing and specialized therapeutic care.

Veterans Affairs Canada (VAC)

Eligible Canadian Veterans and their surviving spouses may qualify for funded in-home care under the Veterans Independence Program (VIP).
