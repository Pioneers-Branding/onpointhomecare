Home Is Where the Heart Is

Remaining in the Home You Love, Safely and Confidently

Over 90% of Canadian seniors express a deep, heartfelt desire to stay in their own homes for the rest of their lives. A home holds decades of cherished memories, familiar neighborhoods, gardens, beloved pets, and established routines that provide profound comfort and cognitive grounding.

However, as mobility changes, chronic health conditions emerge, or memory begins to slip, maintaining a home safely requires strategic support. OnPoint's Aging in Place service makes long-term home living a safe reality. We provide proactive health monitoring, personal care assistance, home safety modifications, and warm companionship that adapt gracefully as your needs change over time.

"Aging in place is not about avoiding change; it is about having the right clinical support in place so you never have to leave the place you love."

A Graduated Model

How Our Aging in Place Care Evolves Over Time

We meet seniors exactly where they are today and scale seamlessly tomorrow:

Phase 1: Light Assistance & Companionship

A few hours per week for grocery trips, meal prep, stimulating social conversation, medication reminders, and garden walks.

Phase 2: Daily Living & Personal Care (ADLs)

Daily morning and evening visits assisting with bathing, dressing, transfers, incontinence care, and nutritious cooking.

Phase 3: Clinical Health & Chronic Care

Registered Nurse oversight for vital signs tracking, diabetes management, complex wound care, and dementia behavioral support.

Phase 4: 24/7 & Overnight Vigilance

Continuous round-the-clock home presence or waking overnight supervision ensuring safety and immediate emergency response.

Comprehensive Services

Key Pillars of Our Aging in Place Program

Holistic support designed to preserve health, dignity, and autonomy.

Pillar 01

Proactive Health Surveillance & Vital Tracking

Preventing hospital visits is central to staying home. Our registered nurses conduct regular vital checks, screen for early infection signs, and track chronic conditions such as hypertension, heart failure, and diabetes.

  • Regular blood pressure, oxygen saturation, and blood glucose logs
  • Early symptom detection (fever, breathlessness, edema, confusion)
  • Direct communication with family physicians and specialists
  • Routine care plan reviews every 90 days
Pillar 02

Dignified Daily Living & Personal Hygiene (ADLs)

Our compassionate care aides assist with personal routines with the utmost respect and patience, preserving personal dignity while ensuring total hygiene and skin integrity.

  • Gentle assistance with showering, sponge baths, and hair care
  • Dressing assistance, footwear inspection, and grooming
  • Discreet incontinence management and skin barrier care
  • Oral hygiene, denture care, and skin hydration
Pillar 03

Nutritious Meal Preparation & Culinary Support

Poor nutrition and dehydration are leading causes of senior decline. We prepare fresh, wholesome, home-cooked meals tailored to dietary preferences, low-sodium requirements, and diabetic needs.

  • Freshly prepared home meals tailored to cultural and medical diets
  • Hydration prompts and fluid balance tracking
  • Grocery shopping, pantry organization, and food freshness audits
  • Pleasant, shared mealtime companionship
Pillar 04

Medication Management & Pharmacy Coordination

We ensure that every prescribed medication is taken accurately at the correct time, preventing missed doses and dangerous adverse drug reactions.

  • Dosette box and blister pack administration supervision
  • Coordination with community pharmacies for timely refills
  • Side effect surveillance and doctor notification
  • Elimination of expired or duplicate medications
Pillar 05

Social Engagement & Cognitive Stimulation

Loneliness and isolation take a heavy toll on cognitive health. Our caregivers provide stimulating conversations, accompany seniors on community outings, and encourage engaging hobbies.

  • Accompaniment to local senior centres, parks, and libraries
  • Engaging puzzles, board games, crafts, and reading
  • Facilitating video calls with grandchildren and distant family
  • Preserving cherished family and spiritual traditions
Pillar 06

Home Safety Audits & Environmental Adaptations

We continuously review the living space to eliminate trip hazards, optimize lighting, and recommend assistive equipment that makes navigating the home effortless.

  • Walkway hazard removal and non-slip mat installation
  • Recommendations for grab bars, stair rails, and transfer poles
  • Optimizing furniture layout for walker and wheelchair clearance
  • Emergency call systems and contact board maintenance
"Home is not just a building; it is a repository of identity and dignity. Our mission is to keep you thriving there safely."
