High-Acuity Nursing

Hospital-Level Clinical Care in the Comfort of Home

When a senior or medically fragile adult requires specialized medical intervention—such as central line maintenance, enteral nutrition, complex surgical wound irrigation, or catheter irrigation—traditional companion care is not enough.

OnPoint’s Complex Home Nursing service bridges the hospital-to-home divide. Directed by Risper Murunga (RN, BSN, MPH), our registered nursing team executes intricate clinical care plans with sterile precision, close physician communication, and vigilant monitoring to keep your loved one medically stable and out of the emergency room.

"High clinical complexity should never mean being confined to a hospital bed. With the right nursing leadership, sophisticated medical care can be safely and warmly delivered at home."

Clinical Scope

High-Acuity Procedures We Manage at Home

Our Registered Nurses and Licensed Practical Nurses are certified in advanced clinical procedures.

IV Therapy & Central Lines

Sterile administration of intravenous medications, hydration, PICC line dressing changes, heparin flushes, and port-a-cath maintenance.

Enteral Nutrition (G-Tube / J-Tube)

Gastrostomy and jejunostomy tube feeding administration, pump calibration, stoma skin barrier protection, and tube patency flushing.

Tracheostomy & Respiratory Care

Airway suctioning, tracheostomy tube cuff checks, inner cannula cleaning, oxygen titration, and nebulizer therapy administration.

Complex Catheter & Ostomy Care

Suprapubic and Foley catheter insertion/irrigation, urinary tract infection monitoring, and colostomy/ileostomy stoma care.

Clinical Governance

How Our Complex Nursing Pathway Works

A rigorous, physician-aligned process ensuring safety and medical stability.

Stage 01

Specialist & Hospital Physician Alignment

Before home nursing starts, our Lead Registered Nurse conducts a thorough clinical review with hospital discharge planners, attending physicians, or community specialists to translate medical directives into home-executable care protocols.

  • Review of laboratory results, imaging, and post-surgical directives
  • Verification of medical supply inventory and infusion pharmacy setup
  • Establishment of physician escalation triggers and emergency action plans
Stage 02

Medical Setup & Home Sterility Audit

We ensure your home is equipped with hospital-grade hygiene standards, proper medical equipment (suction units, infusion pumps, sterile dressing packs), and safe biohazard disposal.

  • Calibration of medical infusion and oxygen delivery devices
  • Placement of sterile barrier fields and PPE supplies
  • Emergency resuscitation and bedside backup supply setup
Stage 03

Licensed Nursing Execution & Health Surveillance

All complex procedures are performed by licensed RNs or LPNs adhering to British Columbia College of Nurses and Midwives (BCCNM) clinical standards.

  • Strict aseptic technique for sterile dressings, lines, and catheterizations
  • Multi-system vital signs assessment (SpO2, blood pressure, blood glucose, temperature)
  • Early symptom detection to prevent sepsis, fluid overload, or acute decline
Stage 04

Continuous Physician Sync & Family Reporting

We provide clear, detailed clinical shift logs to both family members and treating physicians, ensuring all stakeholders are synchronized on patient stability.

  • Real-time digital charting accessible by designated family members
  • Formal nursing summaries provided to primary care providers
  • Regular clinical reassessments to adjust dosages and care intensity
