Restoring balance, emotional well-being, and vital rest for family caregivers caring for aging parents across Metro Vancouver.

Sustainable Family Caregiving

You Cannot Pour From an Empty Cup

Caring for an aging mother, father, or spouse is an act of deep love. But as care needs intensify—incontinence, midnight wandering, complex medication routines, and chronic physical lifting—the burden on family caregivers becomes crushing. Exhaustion sets in, careers suffer, and personal health is quietly neglected.

Caregiver burnout is not a sign of failure or lack of love; it is the natural consequence of doing a multi-person job alone without breaks. OnPoint's Caregiver Support & Respite program steps into the breach. We take over the heavy nursing and daily care tasks on a predictable schedule, allowing you to sleep through the night, take vacations, and enjoy meaningful, loving time with your family again.

"When you step back from being a 24/7 exhausted nurse, you can return to being a loving daughter, son, or partner. That is the true gift of professional respite."

Self-Assessment

Recognizing the Warning Signs of Caregiver Burnout

If you or a family member are experiencing several of these indicators, professional support is urgently needed:

Chronic Exhaustion & Sleep Deprivation

Waking up multiple times each night to assist with bathroom trips or wandering, leaving you in a state of permanent physical fatigue.

Short Temper & Unintended Irritability

Feeling sudden frustration, resentment, or impatience over small repetitive questions, followed by deep feelings of guilt.

Neglecting Personal Health & Needs

Skipping your own doctor appointments, neglecting exercise and healthy meals, and feeling constantly disconnected from friends and hobbies.

Dread of Tomorrow & Helplessness

Waking up with anxiety about the day's care tasks and feeling trapped with no visible end or sustainable path forward.

How We Support Families

Our Caregiver Relief & Respite Framework

A comprehensive system designed to lift the weight from family shoulders.

Service 01

Scheduled Day & Weekend Respite Blocks

We establish regular, dependable blocks of care—whether 4 hours twice a week or full weekend coverage. You can work, run errands, spend time with your children, or simply rest knowing your parent is in expert hands.

  • Reliable scheduling with zero last-minute cancellations
  • Full management of daytime meals, hygiene, and medications
  • Engaging social activities and companionship for the senior
  • Detailed visit summaries delivered upon your return
Service 02

Waking Overnight Respite for Restful Sleep

Nothing breaks a caregiver faster than broken sleep. Our overnight caregivers stay awake throughout the night, monitoring bedroom exits, assisting with nocturnal toileting, and managing agitation.

  • Guaranteed 8 to 12 hours of uninterrupted sleep for family members
  • Immediate physical response to nighttime restlessness or falls
  • Scheduled nighttime medication administration
  • Peace of mind that your loved one is safe throughout the dark hours
Service 03

Taking Over Heavy Clinical & Hygiene Tasks

Many adult children feel deeply uncomfortable or physically strained performing intimate hygiene, bowel care, or wound dressing. Our registered nurses and care aides take over these sensitive clinical duties with total discretion.

  • Dignified bathing, sponge baths, and incontinence care
  • Wound dressing, catheter care, and vital signs monitoring
  • Safe mechanical lift transfers and bed repositioning
  • Protecting the parent-child relationship from clinical strain
Service 04

Vacation & Travel Respite Coverage

Taking a well-deserved family holiday shouldn't mean putting your loved one in an unfamiliar facility. We provide intensive, temporary 24-hour home care coverage so your parent stays comfortably in their home while you travel.

  • Multi-day and multi-week around-the-clock home care
  • Regular photo updates, video calls, and status reports
  • Emergency escalation protocols with designated out-of-town contacts
  • Complete household continuity, grocery management, and security
"Respite is not an admission of defeat. It is the single most effective strategy to ensure high-quality care for your parent over the long term."
