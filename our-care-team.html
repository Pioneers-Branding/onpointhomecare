Clinical Director & Lead Registered Nurse

Risper Murunga, RN, BSN, MPH

20+ Years Clinical Care & Healthcare Leadership Experience

Risper Murunga brings over 20 years of clinical care and healthcare leadership experience to OnPoint Nurse and Home Care. Having served across acute hospital wards, specialized long-term residential care facilities, and outpatient community clinics, Risper ensures that every elder care plan is founded upon rigorous clinical evidence, infection safety, and profound human compassion.

Educational Qualifications

  • Master of Public Health (MPH)
  • Bachelor of Science in Nursing (BSN)

Breadth of Experience

  • Acute care hospital environments
  • Long-term care & geriatric residential units
  • Outpatient clinics & community home health

Key Areas of Clinical Expertise:

Dementia & Cognitive Care Advanced Wound Care Infection Prevention & Control Falls Prevention & Environmental Safety Activities of Daily Living (ADLs) First Aid & Emergency Response

Rigorous Standards

How We Select & Train Our Caregivers

Families trust us with their loved ones because our team members meet strict clinical, moral, and background requirements.

Thorough Background Screening

Comprehensive identity checks, certified credentials verification, and exhaustive reference screening.

Registered Nurse Supervision

Every caregiver operates under direct observation and regular evaluation by Lead Registered Nurse Risper Murunga.

Empathy & Cultural Alignment

We select individuals who demonstrate innate patience, gentle bedside manner, and deep respect for diverse family backgrounds and care preferences.

Continuous Clinical Education

Ongoing training in dementia communication, fall mitigation, infection control, and emergency first-aid protocols.
